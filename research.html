Research

This page compiles the academic reports, poster presentations, and computational notebooks documenting the Sympoietic Art Organism experiment. Initiated at the 2025 Complexity Global School, this study tracks a collaborative art community as a closed dynamical system to observe cultural evolution in real-time.

Our quantitative and qualitative findings are organized below. You can view each resource directly or download the documents:
