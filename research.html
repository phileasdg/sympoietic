Research

This section collects the academic reports, poster presentations, and computational notebooks detailing the Sympoietic Art Organism experiment. Initiated at the 2025 Complexity Global School (organized jointly by the Santa Fe Institute and Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia), this experiment treats a collaborative art community as a closed dynamical system to study cultural evolution dynamics in real-time.

Our quantitative and qualitative findings are compiled below. Click the links to view or download the materials:

Sympoietic Art Organism at NetSci 2026

▶ Research Poster

Title: Growing a Sympoietic Art Organism: An Experiment Tracking the Micro-Dynamics of Cultural Evolution

The official scientific poster presented at the NetSci 2026 conference. It details the three experimental phases, topological consolidation, network degree properties, and mutations of visual motifs across cycles.

▶ Accepted Abstract

Title: Growing a Sympoietic Art Organism: An Experimental Framework for Tracking the Micro-Dynamics of Cultural Evolution

The scientific abstract accepted for presentation at the NetSci 2026 conference. It summarizes the endogenous emergence of "rich-get-richer" preferential attachment and giant component consolidation from the feed-forward DAG of citation edges.

2. CGS 2025 Reports

▶ CGS 2025 Project Report

Title: Complexity Global School 2025 Project Report: Sympoietic Art Organism

The definitive project report outlining the methodology, quantitative network analysis (degree distributions, community detection, clustering coefficients), qualitative semantic phylogeny, and extensive participant feedback surveys.

▶ Computational Explorations Notebook

Title: Art organism computational explorations

A comprehensive 94-page document hosting algorithms, data tables, matrix computations, and network visualizations compiled to audit and analyze the citation patterns of the sympoietic organism.
